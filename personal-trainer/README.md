# Personal Trainer

Install in Claude Code: `/plugin install personal-trainer --marketplace nicoburkart/ai-personal-trainer`. In the Claude desktop app: Plugins → Add marketplace → `nicoburkart/ai-personal-trainer`.

An AI personal trainer that runs inside Claude (Cowork or Claude Code) on your normal Claude plan.

- **Setup:** a few questions about your goal, body, schedule and food. It works out your daily calories and protein and writes your training plan.
- **Plan my week:** meals and a shopping list that hit your numbers, using real nutrition labels from Open Food Facts.
- **Fill my cart:** puts the list in your supermarket's online cart if your store has a connector. You always press order yourself.
- **Check in:** tell it your workouts and weight; it tracks the trend and adjusts your plan.
- **Connect my supermarket:** sets up a connector for Mercadona, Kroger or Picnic, checks it's safe, and switches ordering off.

Everything it knows about you stays in Markdown files in the folder you work in: `profile.md`, `plan.md`, `foods.md`, `week.md`, `log.md`.

It's a starting point: the skills are plain Markdown you can read and change.

Not medical advice. If you have a medical condition, take medication, are pregnant or breastfeeding, are under 18, or have a history of eating disorders, check with a doctor first; the trainer won't set a calorie deficit in those cases.
